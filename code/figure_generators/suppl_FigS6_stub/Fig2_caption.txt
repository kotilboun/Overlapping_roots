Fig. 2 Direct event-level verification of the four terms entering the infectious-population balance (Eq. 2.50). The horizontal axis is the state-supplied expected event count and the vertical axis is the observed ABM event count, both per realization and observation interval, for (a) transmission, (b) spontaneous removal, (c) identification, and (d) traced removal. Each point represents one of the 195 raw-rate protocols and one of 150 observation intervals, averaged over 120 independent realizations at S_0=8000, I_0=160. The dashed line is the identity. Intervals with identically zero tracing intensity are omitted from panel (d) to avoid overplotting at the origin. This is a conditional-intensity and implementation check based on ABM-supplied states, rather than an independent fit to the deterministic trajectory.
