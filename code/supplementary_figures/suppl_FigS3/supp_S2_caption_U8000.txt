Supplementary Figure S2. Finite-population validation of the deterministic hierarchy at the selected production setting S0=8000, I0=160, and 120 ABM realizations per c-value. Columns correspond to c=0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, and 1; rows show I(τ)/S0, S(τ)/S0, m1(τ), and c m1(τ)I(τ)/S0. The ABM uses the canonical dimensional decomposition Γ=1, γ=0, γ_c=1, and p_f=c. Blue points show ABM replicate means with normal-approximation 95% confidence-interval error bars, and orange curves show the numerically converged high-depth deterministic reference computed with K=40. The reference was checked against K=80; the maximum difference in the plotted scaled coordinates was 8.84e-11. The vertical dotted line marks activation of forward tracing at τ=0.5. Because each ABM mean is based on 120 realizations and the population scale is large, the confidence intervals are narrow and may be partly obscured by the markers.
